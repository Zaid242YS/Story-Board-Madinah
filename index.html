Phase 1 — Proof of Concept
Phase 1 · Origin01
A seed in the desert night
A founder stares at a problem: KSA imports 80% of its food. In the silence before dawn, the aquaponics idea takes root.
✦ Feeling: quiet resolve
Phase 1 · Build02
The rooftop pilot is born
Wooden trellises rise above city walls. IBC tanks carry the first fish. Tomato vines climb toward the Arabian sun.
✦ Feeling: hands-on grit
Phase 1 · Growth03
First fruit swells green
Clusters of tomatoes form along every vine — unripe, promising, proof that the system feeds the plant. The fish below are doing their work.
✦ Feeling: patient hope
Phase 1 · Harvest04
Red gold on the counter
Heirloom beefsteaks and sweet cherry tomatoes. The first real harvest. Numbers are recorded. The data package begins.
✦ Feeling: breakthrough joy
Phase 1 · System05
Fish feed the farm
Nile tilapia convert feed to protein and release ammonia-rich water. The plants drink it. Nothing is wasted.
✦ Feeling: wonder at nature
Phase 2 — Scale & Replicate
Phase 2 · Tech06
The super adobe greenhouse
Earthbag walls absorb intense desert heat by day and release it gently at night. No mechanical chillers. No electrical single point of failure.
✦ Feeling: ingenious simplicity
Phase 2 · Scale07
Three domes near Madinah
The pilot model is duplicated. Three interconnected super adobe greenhouses share water and nutrients. Weekly harvests begin.
✦ Feeling: momentum building
Phase 2 · Market08
Fresh to Madinah's hotels
Refrigerated vans carry the morning harvest to hotel kitchens by noon. Chefs post about it. The brand earns its story.
✦ Feeling: pride and purpose
Phase 3 — Vision 2030 Horizon
Phase 3 · Greens09
500 m² of living lettuce
144 heads per m² per month. A dedicated leafy greens house generates SAR 840,000 a year on minimal OPEX.
✦ Feeling: quiet abundance
Phase 3 · Loop10
Alfalfa feeds the herd
Fish effluent irrigates 8,000 m² of alfalfa for just SAR 3,000. The alfalfa fattens cattle. Manure feeds back to the fish. Every waste becomes revenue.
✦ Feeling: elegant circularity
Phase 3 · Expand11
The map turns green
Qaseem supplies Riyadh. Jeddah outskirts feed the Red Sea coast. The same dome, the same system — replicated.
✦ Feeling: national pride
Phase 3 · Vision12
A new dawn for KSA food
Dozens of super adobe domes dot the Kingdom at sunrise. Import dependency fades. A model enterprise becomes national infrastructure.
✦ Feeling: legacy and hope
